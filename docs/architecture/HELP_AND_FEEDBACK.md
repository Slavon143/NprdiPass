# Help and feedback

Authenticated active company members can open `onboarding.help` and `onboarding.support` from the header or sidebar. Help contains practical guides and native, keyboard-accessible FAQ disclosures. Feedback accepts questions, problem reports and suggestions, with a subject up to 120 characters and a message of 10–2000 characters.

`POST /onboarding/feedback` uses CSRF and a five-per-minute authenticated throttle. Tenant and reporter identifiers always come from current company/authentication, never request fields. `SubmitFeedbackAction` checks live company/user/membership authorization under a transaction, writes the message and tenant audit together, and rolls back both on audit failure. A form UUID makes exact repeats idempotent. Reusing it for changed content is rejected; a UUID belonging to another user/company cannot overwrite or expose their record.

Messages use the existing `platform_incidents` store with the reserved `customer_feedback` type, info severity, and question/bug/idea category in JSON context. This avoids a new schema while retaining existing platform resolution. Customer feedback is excluded from operational incident health counts. `feedback.submitted` audits include the record UUID and category, never the subject or message. Free text is escaped on every page.

The customer history is scoped to both selected company and authenticated sender and paginated by ten records. Status is Received for open records and Resolved after a support administrator resolves them. Customer feedback lists twenty records per page and requires an active, verified account, the enabled platform-operations feature, `platform.access` and `platform.feedback.view`. Its dedicated POST resolution endpoint requires `platform.feedback.manage`, reloads and authorizes the actor inside a transaction, locks only a `customer_feedback` record, and writes a platform audit atomically. Repeated resolution is idempotent. Operational incidents cannot be resolved through this endpoint.

The `support_admin` role contains exactly these three permissions. The sidebar displays NordiPass management → Customer feedback only for active accounts with platform access and feedback-view permission, including staff without a selected company. Its badge counts open customer feedback, excludes resolved feedback and operational incidents, and disappears when no requests remain. Compact mode retains the icon, title and count. Company health, company administration and broader platform operations retain separate permissions; the operations overview requires CompaniesView. Platform roles do not bypass membership when submitting a company message or accessing tenant resources. Role creation by the seeder does not automatically assign users. The existing human staging account received this limited role through an explicitly authorized, audited activation on October 8.

Delivery currently stays inside NordiPass. There is no outgoing email, webhook, attachment upload or new provider integration. Email delivery requires a separately selected recipient/configuration. The existing optional company support email remains informational in the recovery section.

## Conversations

The original feedback message is retained. Append-only replies use `feedback_replies` with a unique UUID submission nonce, feedback FK, nullable author FK, server-derived customer/support author type and message text. No edit or delete endpoint is exposed. A conversation displays the most recent twenty replies, rendered chronologically within each page, with pagination to older replies. Customer history orders by last activity; the support inbox prioritizes open requests, then last activity and ID.

`GET /onboarding/feedback/{feedback}` and its POST `/reply` endpoint require the selected company's CompanyView permission, the feedback's company ID and authenticated original sender. Another company member cannot read or continue the sender's conversation. Platform conversation reads require feedback-view permission, and replies require feedback-manage plus platform access. The two POST routes are UUID constrained, CSRF protected and throttled at ten requests per minute; message length is 1–2000 characters. Blank text is rejected and all text is escaped. Client-supplied author/tenant fields never establish context.

`ReplyToFeedbackAction` uses live authorization and company/user/resource locks, serializes appends on the feedback row and atomically stores the reply, safe audit and support-reply notification. Exact reposts are idempotent; nonce reuse for a changed body, author or another conversation is rejected. A new customer reply reopens a resolved request and clears resolution attribution. A support reply preserves the current status. Every new reply updates last activity. Operational incidents cannot use these endpoints. Audits contain UUIDs and author type, never reply text.

A support reply creates one `feedback.reply` in-app notification for the original sender if their company/account is active and membership remains present, respecting notification opt-out/muting. It does not notify all company members or send email. The notification contains generic text and the protected conversation URL. Opening the customer conversation marks only notifications for replies displayed on that page read; notifications for older pages or other conversations/users/companies remain unchanged. No additional platform-role grant is needed.
